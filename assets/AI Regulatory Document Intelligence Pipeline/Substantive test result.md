## Extracted Regulatory Intelligence

### Summary

The Securities and Exchange Commission, Nigeria published the "Guidelines on Revised Minimum Capital for Regulated Entities" on March 18, 2026.

The document establishes a risk-sensitive and proportionate capital framework for Capital Market Operators (CMOs) to ensure adequate financial resources, enhance market resilience, support supervisory intervention, and align with IOSCO principles.

### Obligations Identified

1. Capital Market Operators (CMOs) must maintain adequate financial resources to absorb operational and market-related losses.
2. Regulated entities must meet clear capital adequacy standards to support effective supervision and early regulatory intervention.

### Affected Entities

1. Capital Market Operators (CMOs)
2. Regulated entities under the Securities and Exchange Commission, Nigeria

### Important Dates

1. **2026-03-18** — Publication date of the Guidelines on Revised Minimum Capital for Regulated Entities.

### Compliance Risks

1. Inability to meet undisclosed revised minimum capital thresholds because specific amounts were not available in the extracted text.
2. Failure to maintain adequate financial resources to absorb operational and market-related losses.
3. Exposure to early regulatory intervention for non-compliance with capital adequacy standards.

### Required Actions

1. Review existing capital adequacy against the revised guidelines.
2. Maintain financial resources sufficient to absorb operational and market-related losses.
3. Obtain and review the full PDF document, "Guidelines on New Capital Base CMOS 2026.pdf", for specific minimum capital amounts, implementation timelines, and detailed compliance requirements.

### Recommended Action

Review identified regulatory obligations, risks and required actions.
