# Project Ascension — Regulatory Intelligence Portfolio

## Portfolio Overview

This portfolio demonstrates the design and implementation of an AI-enabled regulatory intelligence capability for organizations operating in complex regulated environments.

The portfolio combines two complementary systems:

1. **Global Regulatory Intelligence Watchtower**
2. **AI Regulatory Document Intelligence Pipeline**

Together, these projects demonstrate an end-to-end regulatory intelligence workflow:

> **Discover → Monitor → Ingest → Process → Structure → Analyze → Alert → Support Decision-Making**

The systems are designed to help regulated teams identify regulatory developments, process regulatory documents, extract structured intelligence, and surface material changes through repeatable workflows.

---

## 1. Portfolio Objective

The objective of this portfolio is to show and demonstrate practical capability in:

- Regulatory intelligence operations
- AI-assisted document intelligence
- Workflow automation
- Structured information extraction
- Regulatory change monitoring
- Compliance-oriented data pipelines
- Evidence-based operational reporting
- AI workflow architecture
- Project and process design

Please Note: The portfolio is focused on **operational regulatory intelligence**, not the provision of legal advice.

---

# 2. Portfolio Architecture

The two projects address different stages of the regulatory intelligence lifecycle.

### Project 1 — Global Regulatory Intelligence Watchtower

The Watchtower focuses primarily on **regulatory discovery and monitoring**.

It is designed to identify relevant regulatory developments across jurisdictions and surface changes that may require further review.

### Project 2 — AI Regulatory Document Intelligence Pipeline

The Document Intelligence Pipeline focuses primarily on **regulatory document processing and structured intelligence extraction**.

It is designed to process regulatory documents and convert unstructured regulatory information into structured, reviewable intelligence.
